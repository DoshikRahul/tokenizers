## Subjective
Chief Complaint: Injured ankle while playing basketball with son
History of Present Illness (HPI): The patient, a 45-year-old male, presents with a swollen, bruised, and painful ankle after twisting it while playing basketball with his son a few days ago.

## Objective

There is pain on pressure on the outside of the ankle, and the ankle feels unstable.
The patient is unable to bear weight on the ankle.
There is no obvious deformity or skin changes.
The X-ray of the ankle reveals a displaced lateral malleolus fracture.
The lateral malleolus fracture is at the Weber C level.

## Assessment and Plan
The patient has a displaced lateral malleolus fracture.
The fracture is at the Weber C level.
The fracture is causing ankle instability.
The fracture is causing pain.
