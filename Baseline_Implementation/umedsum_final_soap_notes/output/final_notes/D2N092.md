# D2N092 - uMedSum final note

## Subjective
Chief Complaint: Arm hurting near the wrist.
History of Present Illness (HPI): The patient, a 43-year-old female, presents with a complaint of arm pain near the wrist.
She reports that the pain started after hitting the pole of the net while playing tennis, and it has been swollen and painful since then.
She denies numbness, tingling, or weakness in her hand or fingers.
She has no prior history of wrist or arm injuries or conditions.

## Objective
Physical Examination & Vitals: The patient's physical examination revealed swelling on the part of the arm near the wrist, tenderness on the part of the arm near the wrist, and stiffness, especially when moving the wrist or flexing the thumb.
Vital signs were not mentioned.
The patient's X-ray result was normal.

## Assessment and Plan (Stage 1, unchanged)
* **Diagnoses and Clinical Reasoning:** Based on the patient's symptoms and physical examination findings, it is likely that the patient has a wrist strain or sprain. Further evaluation and imaging studies may be necessary to confirm the diagnosis.
* **Treatment Plan, Medications, and Follow-up:** The patient will be prescribed a course of physical therapy to address the stiffness and pain in her wrist. She will also be advised to avoid activities that exacerbate the condition and to use ice and compression to reduce swelling. Follow-up appointments will be scheduled to monitor the patient's progress and adjust the treatment plan as necessary.

Please note that this is a sample SOAP note, and the actual diagnosis and treatment plan may vary depending on the patient's specific condition and the provider's clinical judgment.
