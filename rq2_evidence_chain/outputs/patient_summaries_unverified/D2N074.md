## Why you came in
You came in because you've been having headaches for a few months. The headaches are behind your eyes and feel like a dull, nagging ache. They have gotten worse over time, from a 3 out of 10 to a 6 out of 10. They are worse in the morning and last a few hours. ## What it may be
You may have a pituitary mass pressing on the optic chiasm. This can cause vision loss and headaches. You have a condition called bitemporal hemianopia, which means you are losing vision on the sides of your vision field. Your TSH was low, but other pituitary hormone levels were normal.
