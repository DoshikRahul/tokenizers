# Visit summary from the UNVERIFIED note (H3 control) — D2N112

## Why you came in
You came in because your back pain has gotten worse over time. The pain is in your lower back and goes down your left leg. It feels like an electrical toothache and is very strong, up to 13 out of 10 when it's worst. You use a cane because it's hard to stand for long periods.

## What it may be
You had a fall off a roof 30 years ago, which might have caused a back injury. You're having weakness in both legs and drag your foot when walking. Your doctor thinks the pain may be from a disk pressing on a nerve in your lower back.

## What happens next
The doctor will check with your primary care doctor or a radiologist to see if there is spinal narrowing at the L4-5 level. They will also find out which nerve is affected by the disk problem. The next steps will be discussed after getting more information.
