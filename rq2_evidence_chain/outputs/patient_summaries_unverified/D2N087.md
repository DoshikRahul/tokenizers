## Why you came in
You came in because you have a tick bite around your knee that has been burning and annoying you for a week.

## What we found
You have a bull's-eye rash around the bite area, with some redness and swelling. You don't have pain or other symptoms like fever or chills, but you feel a little unwell.

## What it may be
The tick bite and bull's-eye rash could be a sign of Lyme disease or another tick-borne illness.
