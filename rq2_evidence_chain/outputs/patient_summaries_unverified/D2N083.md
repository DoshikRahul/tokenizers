# Visit summary from the UNVERIFIED note (H3 control) — D2N083

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been having pain on the inside of your right elbow for four days. The pain gets worse when you move your wrist or do activities like pottery or lifting.

## What we found
We found that you have tenderness around the inside of your elbow, specifically near the medial epicondyle. There is no swelling, redness, or numbness in your elbow or forearm.

## What it may be
This may be a condition called medial epicondylitis, which is pain and tenderness on the inside of the elbow often caused by repetitive strain or overuse.

## What happens next
The doctor will check your medications and may refer you to physical therapy. You should rest your elbow and avoid activities that make the pain worse. A follow-up appointment will be scheduled to check on your progress.
