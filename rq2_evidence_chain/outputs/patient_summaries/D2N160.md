# Your visit summary — D2N160

## Why you came in
You came in because you have sharp pain in your right elbow that started a week ago after playing lacrosse with your kids.

## What we found

## What it may be
The pain is likely from a soft tissue injury or damage to the ligaments in your elbow.

## What happens next
