# Your visit summary — D2N129

## Why you came in
You came in for your yearly check-up and to talk about your diabetes.

## What we found
You are 24 years old and have type 2 diabetes. You are taking metformin as prescribed, but your blood sugar levels have been up and down lately.

## What it may be
Your diabetes may not be well-controlled because of your new job at a bakery, which has made it harder to manage your snacks and blood sugar levels.

## What happens next
Your doctor will review your medication and help you find ways to better manage your diabetes at work. You should keep track of your blood sugar levels and share any changes with your doctor.
