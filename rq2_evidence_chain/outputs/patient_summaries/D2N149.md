# Your visit summary — D2N149

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been coughing up blood, about three to four cups each day, and you have been short of breath for a few weeks. You also mentioned you have lost about 10 pounds without trying and feel tired and have less of an appetite.

## What we found
We found that you are a 57-year-old male who has not had any imaging tests like a chest X-ray or CT scan. You have not had any chest pain or fever, but you have been having trouble getting appointments for blood tests or other care.

## What happens next
The doctor will arrange for you to have imaging tests like a chest X-ray or CT scan to look for the cause of the blood coughing. Blood tests such as a complete blood count, coagulation profile, or tumor markers will also be done to check for possible issues with blood clotting or anemia. You will need to come back for a follow-up after the results of these tests are available.
