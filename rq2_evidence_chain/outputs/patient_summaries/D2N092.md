# Your visit summary — D2N092

## Why you came in
You came in because you have swelling and pain in your wrist after hitting a pole while playing tennis.

## What we found
You have tenderness near your wrist, especially when pressing on the area. The pain gets worse when you move your wrist or bend your thumb.

## What it may be
You likely have a wrist injury from the recent trauma. You do not have numbness, tingling, or weakness in your hand or fingers.

## What happens next
The doctor will look at the X-ray results. You will need to come back in one week for a follow-up. You should apply ice and take over-the-counter pain medicine as needed. Avoid heavy lifting or bending to prevent making the injury worse.
