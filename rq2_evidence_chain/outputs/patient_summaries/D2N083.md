# Your visit summary — D2N083

## Why you came in
You came in because you have pain on the inside of your right elbow that has been getting worse over the past four days.

## What we found
You described the pain as a dull ache that can become sharp when you move your wrist or use your arm against resistance.

## What it may be
You have tenderness around the inside of your elbow, near the medial epicondyle, which is a bony part of the elbow.

## What happens next
The doctor will check your medications and may refer you to physical therapy. You will need to rest your elbow and avoid activities that make the pain worse. A follow-up appointment will be scheduled to check on your progress.
