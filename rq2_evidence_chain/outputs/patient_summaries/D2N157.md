# Your visit summary — D2N157

## Why you came in
You came in to check your overall health and ask if you need to take any extra steps.

## What we found
You have mild leg puffiness and feel dizzy when standing up quickly. Your blood pressure drops by 15/10 mmHg when you stand up.

## What it may be
The dizziness may be due to your blood pressure dropping when you stand up, called orthostatic hypotension.

## What happens next
You will keep checking your blood pressure and report any changes. You should drink more fluids and keep taking your medicines as prescribed.
