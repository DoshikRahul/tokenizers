# Your visit summary — D2N124

## Why you came in
You came in because your ankle is swollen, bruised, and painful. You can stand on it but can't walk due to instability.

## What we found
We found swelling on the outside of your ankle and tenderness on both the outside and inside. X-rays showed a broken bone on the lateral side of your ankle.

## What it may be
You have a broken bone on the outside of your ankle, called a displaced lateral malleolus fracture at the Weber C level.

## What happens next
The doctor will talk to you about next steps, which may include surgery and physical therapy.
