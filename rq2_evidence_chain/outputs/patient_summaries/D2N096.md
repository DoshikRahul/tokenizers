# Your visit summary — D2N096

## Why you came in
You came in because you have left shoulder pain that started after a gymnastics class where you fell into an awkward position.

## What we found
You said the pain is in your left shoulder and hurts when you move. You have not taken any pain medication except for Tylenol, which did not help. You have trouble sleeping on the affected side and have difficulty with daily activities. You have not had any swelling, bruising, numbness, tingling, or weakness in your left arm or hand.

## What it may be
You have acute left shoulder pain following the injury from your gymnastics class.

## What happens next
The doctor will need to perform a physical examination and possibly order imaging studies, such as X-rays or an MRI, to further assess the injury. You will need to follow up with the doctor to discuss the results of any imaging studies and to receive further guidance on managing the pain and improving function.
