# Your visit summary — D2N136

## Why you came in
You came in because you have pain in both knees, especially the left one, which started after you began doing stair workouts.

## What we found
We found that your knees are swollen, red, and stiff. You have morning stiffness and the pain gets worse with activity. There is fluid buildup, especially on the left knee. Your x-rays of the left knee were normal.

## What it may be
The pain, swelling, and redness may be related to your stair workouts. We will check for possible autoimmune issues with some blood tests.

## What happens next
You will keep monitoring your blood pressure and report any changes. We will check your knees again in the future to see if there are any changes or if you need more help.
