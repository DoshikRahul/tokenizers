# Your visit summary — D2N120

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had lower back pain for two years. The pain is sharp and stabbing, and it goes down your right leg. It causes numbness and tingling.

## What we found
We did not find any results from tests or exams during this visit. You have not had an EMG, nerve conduction studies, or imaging of your lower back.

## What it may be
You may have a herniated disk at the L4-L5 level. This can press on a nerve, causing pain, numbness, and tingling in your leg.

## What happens next
You are willing to have more tests, like EMG and nerve conduction studies. These tests will help us better understand which nerve is affected and guide your treatment.
