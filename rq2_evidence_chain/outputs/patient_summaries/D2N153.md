# Your visit summary — D2N153

## Why you came in
You came in because you hurt your right knee about two and a half weeks ago when you stepped off a curb and twisted your ankle. You feel a clicking on the outside of your knee when you squat all the way or rest your foot on your heel for a long time. You also feel like your knee gets locked up when you rest your foot on your heel, but it doesn't hurt much.

## What we found
An x-ray of your knee showed no broken bones or bony problems. You don't have any swelling, instability, or trouble moving your knee fully.

## What it may be
Your symptoms may be from a soft tissue injury or a strain in your ligaments. We don't know exactly what caused your injury yet.

## What happens next
If your symptoms don't get better, we may consider an MRI to look for problems like a meniscal tear or ligament injury. We will talk about the next steps if your symptoms don't improve.
