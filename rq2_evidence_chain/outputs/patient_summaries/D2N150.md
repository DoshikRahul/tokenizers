# Your visit summary — D2N150

## Why you came in
You came in because your right knee feels loose and unstable. You injured it three days ago while playing soccer. You heard a pop on the inside of your knee when you tried to plank kick the ball.

## What we found
Your knee is tender on the inside, especially when pressed. You have pain when bending or straightening your knee. You can still walk, but it feels less stable.

## What it may be
You may have an injury to the anterior cruciate ligament, or ACL. This is a ligament that helps keep your knee stable.

## What happens next
You will have an MRI to check the ACL. Your doctor will talk to you about the results and next steps after the test.
