# Your visit summary — D2N180

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been feeling numbness and tingling in both hands for a few weeks. This started after a car wreck in December when you broke both wrists. You also have mild stiffness in your neck, which was diagnosed as a contusion.

## What we found
You have not had any imaging studies for your neck or wrists since the accident. You are willing to have an MRI of the cervical spine and X-rays of the wrists to check for nerve compression or spinal issues.

## What it may be
The numbness and tingling may be related to nerve compression or cervical radiculopathy. You are also open to having electromyography (EMG) or nerve conduction studies to check for peripheral nerve involvement.

## What happens next
You are willing to have further tests to evaluate your condition.
