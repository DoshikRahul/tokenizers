# Your visit summary — D2N201

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had back pain for 2 years, which started after a fall playing basketball. You say the pain is 7-8 out of 10 and radiates down your leg, causing numbness in your toes.

## What we found
You report that injections and pain medication did not help much. You have numbness and tingling in your toes and left leg, which feels like pins-and-needles. You also have weakness in your left leg when standing for a long time.

## What it may be
Your MRI shows back issues, including disk desiccation and herniation at L4-L5, which can cause pain and numbness in your legs. You also had one episode of bowel incontinence, which may have been due to dehydration or standing up too quickly.
