# Your visit summary — D2N098

## Why you came in
You came because you have had pain in your left shoulder and elbow for about five months. The pain feels like a constant ache that can get sharper with certain movements. You also have trouble lifting heavy weights, which is different from your usual ability.

## What we found
We found that you have limited movement in your left arm, especially when raising it overhead or twisting it. There is no swelling, redness, numbness, or tingling in your arm.

## What it may be
The pain may be related to a fall you had five months ago while walking up icy stairs. You have not had any tests or exams done yet to confirm the cause.

## What happens next
You will need to follow up with a specialist for more treatment and management.
