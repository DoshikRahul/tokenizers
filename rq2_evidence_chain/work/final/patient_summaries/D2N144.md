# Your visit summary — D2N144

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been having swelling and pain in your right leg, especially around your knee, for about two months. The swelling has gotten worse and now affects your whole leg.

## What we found
We found that your leg is swollen and sore when pressed. You also have warmth and tenderness around your knee. There is no redness or signs of infection.

## What it may be
Your symptoms may be related to your rheumatoid arthritis.

## What happens next
You will need more tests, like imaging of your knee and leg, to check for problems like fluid buildup or other issues. You should keep taking your medicines and talk to your doctor about these next steps.
