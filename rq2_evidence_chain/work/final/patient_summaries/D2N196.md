# Your visit summary — D2N196

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been having blood pressure that goes up and down, along with feelings of lightheadedness and headaches.

## What we found
We found that you have a systolic ejection murmur, which is a heart sound that can be heard with a stethoscope.

## What it may be
Your blood pressure changes may be related to your history of high blood pressure, and we need more tests to understand the cause.

## What happens next
You will come back in a few weeks to get test results and have an echocardiogram, which is a heart ultrasound, to check for any heart issues.
