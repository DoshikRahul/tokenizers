# Your visit summary — D2N095

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had lower back pain for 6 days. The pain feels stiff and constant, and it gets worse when you bend or straighten your back. You say the pain started while you were walking.

## What we found
We did not find any changes in your bowel or bladder function. You do not have numbness, tingling, or weakness in your legs. You have been lifting weights a few times a week, but no recent heavy lifting or trauma.

## What it may be
Your back pain may be related to your weightlifting routine.

## What happens next
You will continue to take ibuprofen and use heat as needed. You should modify your weightlifting routine to avoid making the pain worse. You will have a follow-up appointment to check on your pain and see if more treatment is needed.
