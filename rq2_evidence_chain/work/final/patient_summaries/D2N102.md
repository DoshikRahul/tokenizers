# Your visit summary — D2N102

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had blood in your urine and pain for about six weeks.

## What we found
We found a 6 mm stone in your left kidney. Your urine test showed a small amount of blood, but nothing else was wrong.

## What it may be
You may have kidney stones. You passed some stones recently, which caused the blood and pain.

## What happens next
You will keep track of your urine output and how often you go to the bathroom. You will be seen again to check on your progress.
