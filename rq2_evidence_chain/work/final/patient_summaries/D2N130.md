# Your visit summary — D2N130

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had pain and swelling in your left elbow for three weeks. The pain gets worse when you type and feels better with rest and ice.

## What we found
You have stiffness and limited movement in your left elbow. You do not have morning stiffness or numbness in your arm.

## What it may be
Your symptoms may be from repetitive strain or overuse from typing.

## What happens next
An X-ray of your left elbow will be ordered to check for any bone issues. You will be referred to physical therapy to help with stiffness and movement. You will have a follow-up appointment to check on your progress.
