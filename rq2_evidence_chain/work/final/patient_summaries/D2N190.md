# Your visit summary — D2N190

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had a constant ache in your right knee since a fall two weeks ago. The pain is on the inside of your knee, along the medial joint line, and it is tender to touch. You feel some instability and shifting when walking, but no locking, catching, or giving way of the knee.

## What we found
X-rays showed no significant abnormalities. Ligament testing showed a little laxity in the posterior drawer test, but the anterior drawer test was negative.

## What it may be
You have a soft clicking sensation in your knee when bending or straightening it. The clicking is not accompanied by difficulty in bending or straightening, but you may feel stiff, especially in the morning.

## What happens next
The doctor will review the MRI results and discuss the findings with you. The best course of treatment based on the MRI results will be discussed with you.
