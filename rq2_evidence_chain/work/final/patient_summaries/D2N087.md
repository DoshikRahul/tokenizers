# Your visit summary — D2N087

## Why you came in
You came in because you got a tick bite around your knee a week ago. It has been burning and annoying you.

## What we found
You have a bull's-eye rash around the bite area, with some redness and swelling. You don't have pain or trouble walking.

## What it may be
This could be a sign of Lyme disease or another tick-borne illness. You feel generally unwell but don't have fever, chills, or headaches.

## What happens next
The doctor will check you again and may do tests to find out more. You should watch the rash and tell them if it changes or gets worse.
