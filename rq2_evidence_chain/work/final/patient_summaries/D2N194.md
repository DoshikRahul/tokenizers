# Your visit summary — D2N194

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had left shoulder pain for a few weeks. The pain is constant, dull, and aching, and gets worse when you move, lift things, or sleep on your left side.

## What we found
You recently did work on your basement but did not have any direct injury to your shoulder. You have been taking Tylenol, which helps a little, but the pain is still there. There is no swelling, redness, numbness, or tingling in your shoulder. You have not had any previous injuries or treatments for this issue.

## What it may be
You have chronic left shoulder pain that is made worse by movement and sleeping on your left side.

## What happens next
An MRI will be ordered to look more closely at your left shoulder. You will continue taking Tylenol as needed for pain relief. Your doctor will review your medications and medical history and discuss the MRI results with you.
