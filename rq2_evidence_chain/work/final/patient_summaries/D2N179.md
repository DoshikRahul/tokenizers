# Your visit summary — D2N179

## Why you came in
You came in because you have numbness and tingling in both hands. These symptoms started after a car wreck in December when you broke both wrists. They have gotten worse over the last month.

## What we found
You wear splints on both wrists, and the right one feels worse. You have not taken your nerve pain medicine as prescribed. You have not had any tests like an MRI or EMG to check your nerves. You also have some weakness in your hands and trouble with fine motor tasks.

## What it may be
Your symptoms may be related to nerve issues from the wrist fractures. You have not seen a neurologist or orthopedic specialist for these symptoms.

## What happens next
You are scheduled for a nerve test to check how bad the nerve damage is. After the test, your doctor will refer you to a hand clinic or orthopedist for further care.
