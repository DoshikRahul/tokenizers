# Your visit summary — D2N111

## Why you came in
You came in because you hurt your right knee one week ago at a trampoline park. You have sharp pain on the outside of your knee that gets worse when you walk or bend it. You also have bruising and feel your knee is unstable, making it hard to walk.

## What it may be
The injury may be related to a ligament or meniscus, but we need more tests to be sure.

## What happens next
We are waiting for the results of your X-ray and MRI. Your doctor will review the results and talk to you about what they mean. You will need to work with the radiology team to get the results quickly. Once we have the results, your doctor will decide on the next steps for your care.
