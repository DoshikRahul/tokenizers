# Your visit summary — D2N112

## Why you came in
You came to see us because your back pain has gotten worse. It is in your lower back and goes down your left leg. You have been using a cane because it is hard to stand for long periods.

## What we found
We did not do any tests or exams during this visit. However, you told us that an MRI showed a disk herniation at the L4-5 level. This means a disk in your lower back is pressing on a nerve.

## What it may be
Your symptoms may be caused by a disk herniation at the L4-5 level. This can lead to pain, weakness, and foot dragging. You mentioned you had a fall off a roof 30 years ago, but you did not have surgery then.

## What happens next
We will ask your primary care doctor or the radiologist to check if spinal stenosis is present at the L4-5 level. We also want to know which specific nerve roots are affected by the herniation.
