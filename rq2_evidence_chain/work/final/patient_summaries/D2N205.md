# Your visit summary — D2N205

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been having trouble breathing more than usual and pain when swallowing. This has been getting worse over the weekend and is making it hard to do daily activities.

## What we found
During the exam, we heard crackles in your lungs, which might mean there is fluid there. We did not find any swollen lymph nodes in your neck.

## What it may be
Your symptoms may be related to radiation-induced lung injury and esophagitis. These are conditions that can happen after cancer treatments and can cause trouble breathing and pain when swallowing.

## What happens next
We will think about ordering tests like an endoscopy or a barium swallow to look at your esophagus and check for any problems. You will keep using lidocaine for pain relief and we will watch how your symptoms change.
