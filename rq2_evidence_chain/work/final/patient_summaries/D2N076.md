# Your visit summary — D2N076

## Why you came in
You came because Katherine has Smith-Magenis syndrome, a genetic condition caused by a chromosomal deletion. You mentioned she had trouble walking and sitting by age one, and she has had feeding, occupational, and physical therapy.

## What we found
We learned that Katherine urinates 8 to 10 times a day with a high volume of wet diapers. She drinks a lot of fluids, including pea protein milk and water with some juice.

## What it may be
Smith-Magenis syndrome may be linked to kidney issues, even though there are no known family history of kidney problems or genetic disorders.

The next step is for Katherine to have an ultrasound of her kidneys, as recommended by the geneticist.
