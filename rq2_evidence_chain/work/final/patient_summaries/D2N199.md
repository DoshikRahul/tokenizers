# Your visit summary — D2N199

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had a cough for about a week that has gotten worse in the last two days. You are bringing up yellow sputum and feel unwell.

## What we found
You twisted your right knee when coughing and fell down the stairs, causing bruising and pain on the inside of the knee. You can walk on the knee but have pain and a slight limp. Your temperature is normal, and you are getting enough oxygen.

## What it may be
You have a productive cough with yellow sputum and a recent knee injury with bruising and pain.

## What happens next
The doctor will order a chest X-ray to check the cause of your cough and a right knee X-ray to evaluate the injury. You should follow up with the doctor if your symptoms get worse.
