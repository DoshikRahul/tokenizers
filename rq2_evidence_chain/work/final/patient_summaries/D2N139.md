# Your visit summary — D2N139

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been having palpitations and high blood pressure. You were in the emergency room a few months ago and have been managing your blood pressure with medicine and lifestyle changes.

## What we found
Your blood pressure at home has been around 146-155 mmHg. You are currently taking Benicar HCT (40-25) and amlodipine (10 mg) daily. You have a family history of hypertension, but no specific information about heart disease or endocrine disorders.

## What it may be
You had an episode of palpitations in March, which you think was a panic attack. The palpitations were triggered by stress, drinking a lot of coffee, and skipping breakfast. You did not have any other symptoms like chest pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath during the palpitations.

## What happens next
You will continue to monitor your blood pressure at home and report any changes to your healthcare provider. You will discuss the possibility of cardiac testing, such as an ECG or Holter monitor, with your healthcare provider. You will also discuss the possibility of thyroid function tests or imaging studies with your healthcare provider.
