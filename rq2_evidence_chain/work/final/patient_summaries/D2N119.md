# Your visit summary — D2N119

## Why you came in
You came because you have been having asthma symptoms for 6 months, including flare-ups, coughing, and wheezing. You said you have been seeing doctors often and using a Ventolin inhaler daily and at night. You mentioned that getting sick and being around your aunt's cat make your asthma worse.

## What we found
We found that you have asthma, but you have not had allergy testing or pulmonary function tests. You have not seen a pulmonologist or allergist for asthma management.

## What it may be
Your symptoms match asthma, which includes wheezing, coughing, and chest tightness. Your asthma is not well-controlled because you rely on your rescue inhaler and have not had proper evaluation.

## What happens next
You will be referred to a pulmonologist or allergist for further evaluation. You will have allergy testing to find out what might be causing your asthma flares. You will be given controller medications to help manage your asthma. You will be advised to avoid your aunt's cat and other possible allergens. You will have follow-up appointments to check how well your asthma is controlled.
