# Your visit summary — D2N070

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had lower back pain since Saturday morning.

## What we found
Your pain is in the middle to lower part of your back. It is a 6 or 7 out of 10. It gets worse when you move or sit for a long time, but feels better when you lie on your right side. You feel some tingling in your right toe at first. You have tried Tylenol, ibuprofen, and an icy heat pack, but they haven't helped much.

## What it may be
Your pain may be from a strain, possibly from lifting heavy boxes. You also have a history of type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, and osteoarthritis in your knee.

## What happens next
The doctor will check your medications and may suggest physical therapy or chiropractic care. You should keep using the icy heat pack and avoid heavy lifting or bending. You will have a follow-up appointment in one week to check on your pain and decide on more treatment options.
