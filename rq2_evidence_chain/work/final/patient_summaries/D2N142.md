# Your visit summary — D2N142

## Why you came in
You came in for a follow-up on your high blood pressure and to talk about a shot to help with your drinking.

## What we found
You are taking medicine for high blood pressure and have been checking your blood pressure at home. You drink hard alcohol four to five times a week and find it hard to stop on your own.

## What it may be
You have high blood pressure and a history of drinking alcohol regularly. You are interested in using a shot form of naltrexone, which you prefer over pills because it cannot be easily reversed.

## What happens next
The doctor will talk about starting the shot to help with your drinking. You will need to be watched for any side effects or if it does not work. The doctor may also suggest seeing a specialist for help with your alcohol use.
