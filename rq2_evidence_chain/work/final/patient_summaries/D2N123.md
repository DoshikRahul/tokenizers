# Your visit summary — D2N123

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been having trouble swallowing solid foods like sandwiches for about 4 to 5 weeks. You feel like something is stuck in your throat when you try to swallow.

## What we found
There were no signs of pain, weight loss, or other issues related to your throat or chest.

## What it may be
Your symptoms could be due to a problem with how your throat or esophagus works. This could be something like a blockage or a problem with the muscles that help you swallow.

## What happens next
The doctor will arrange for you to have an upper endoscopy or a barium swallow study. These tests will help check for any structural or muscle-related issues that might be causing your trouble swallowing.
