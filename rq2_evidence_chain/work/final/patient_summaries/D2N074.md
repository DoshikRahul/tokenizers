# Your visit summary — D2N074

## Why you came in
You came in because you have been having headaches behind your eyes for a few months. The headaches are worse in the morning and have gotten more severe over time.

## What we found
We found that you have a pituitary mass pressing on the optic chiasm. This is causing vision loss, which is why you have been bumping into door frames. You also have a low TSH level, but other pituitary hormone levels are normal.

## What it may be
The pituitary mass is significant enough to affect your vision and cause headaches. You may have a condition called bitemporal hemianopia, which means you are losing vision in the outer parts of each eye.

## What happens next
Visual field testing is scheduled before surgery. The exact date of the test is not known yet, and the exact size of the pituitary mass from the MRI is also not known.
