# Visit summary from the UNVERIFIED note (H3 control) — D2N124

## Why you came in
You came in because you hurt your ankle while playing basketball. You twisted it and now it is swollen, bruised, and painful.

## What we found
We found swelling on the outside of your ankle. There is tenderness on both the outside and inside of your ankle. You have pain when we touch the outside of your ankle. There is no obvious deformity, and your skin looks normal.

## What it may be
You may have a broken bone on the outside of your ankle. This is called a displaced lateral malleolus fracture at the Weber C level. This type of break can cause instability and pain.

## What happens next
The doctor will talk to you about next steps. This may include surgery and physical therapy.
