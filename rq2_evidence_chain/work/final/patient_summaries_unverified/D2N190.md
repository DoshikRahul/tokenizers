# Visit summary from the UNVERIFIED note (H3 control) — D2N190

## Why you came in
You came in because you have had a constant ache in your right knee since a fall two weeks ago.

## What we found
Your knee is tender on the inside along the medial joint line. You feel instability and shifting when walking. X-rays showed no significant problems, but there was a little laxity in the posterior drawer test.

## What it may be
You may have an injury to the posterior cruciate ligament in your right knee. This could be causing your pain and feeling of instability.

## What happens next
You will have an MRI to check for possible meniscus tear or cartilage damage. The doctor will look at the results and talk to you about what they mean and next steps.
