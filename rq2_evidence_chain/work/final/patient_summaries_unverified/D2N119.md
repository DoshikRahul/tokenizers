# Visit summary from the UNVERIFIED note (H3 control) — D2N119

## Why you came in
You came because you have had asthma symptoms for 6 months, including flare-ups, coughing, and wheezing. You use a Ventolin inhaler daily and at night when your symptoms get worse. You said getting sick and being around your aunt's cat make your asthma symptoms worse.

## What it may be
You have asthma, which is a condition that makes it hard to breathe and causes coughing and wheezing. Your asthma is not well-controlled, which is why you rely on your rescue inhaler and have not seen a specialist.

## What happens next
You will be referred to a pulmonologist or allergist for further evaluation and management of your asthma. You will have allergy testing to find out if allergens like your aunt's cat are making your asthma worse. You will be prescribed controller medications to help manage your asthma and reduce your need for the rescue inhaler. You will be advised to avoid your aunt's cat and other potential allergens. You will have follow-up appointments to check how well your asthma is controlled and adjust your treatment as needed.
