# Stage 1 generated SOAP - D2N190

## Subjective

* Patient reported a constant ache in the right knee, especially with walking or going up hills, which has been present since a fall two weeks ago.
* The pain is located on the inside of the knee, along the medial joint line, and is tender to touch.
* The patient has not experienced any locking, catching, or giving way of the knee, but has noticed a feeling of instability and shifting when walking.
* The pain worsens with weight-bearing activities and improves with rest.
* The patient has not noticed any swelling, stiffness, or difficulty bearing weight on the knee.

## Objective

* X-rays were taken, which showed no significant abnormalities.
* The doctor performed ligament testing, including the posterior drawer test, which showed a little laxity in the posterior drawer.
* The anterior drawer test was negative.

## Assessment

* The patient is likely experiencing a possible posterior cruciate ligament injury, which may be causing the symptoms of pain, instability, and shifting in the right knee.
* Further evaluation with an MRI is recommended to assess for possible meniscus tear or cartilage damage.

## Plan

* The patient will undergo an MRI to evaluate for possible meniscus tear or cartilage damage.
* The doctor will review the MRI results and discuss the findings with the patient.
* The patient will be advised on the best course of treatment based on the MRI results.
